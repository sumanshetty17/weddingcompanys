Bengaluru is one of India’s top wedding destinations, nestled in lush greenery and filled with picturesque sights. From luxury hotels and elegant banquet halls to scenic resorts, outdoor lawns, and traditional Kalyan Mandapams, the city offers wedding venues suited to every celebration style and budget.

Whether you are planning an intimate ceremony, a grand multi-day wedding, or a destination-style celebration near the city outskirts, Bengaluru has venue options across popular locations like Nandi Hills, Devanahalli, Whitefield, and Kanakapura Road to match every guest count and wedding vision. Best Types Of Wedding Venues In Bengaluru For Your Wedding


Bengaluru offers a wide variety of wedding venues suited to different celebration styles, guest capacities, and budgets. Whether you are planning a traditional South Indian wedding, a luxury destination-style event, or an intimate modern gathering, the city has venue formats to match every requirement.

  • Kalyan Mandapams: Ideal for traditional weddings with cultural rituals, spacious dining areas, and classic mandap setups commonly preferred for South Indian ceremonies.


  • Heritage Properties: Perfect for couples looking for regal architecture, timeless charm, and a more royal-style wedding atmosphere with distinctive character.


  • Wedding Resorts: Best suited for residential and multi-day celebrations, offering accommodation, outdoor lawns, banquet spaces, and destination-style experiences within or around the city.


  • Farmhouses & Lawn Venues: Popular for open-air celebrations, intimate functions, and nature-inspired weddings with flexible decor and event layouts.


  • 5-Star Hotels: A preferred choice for luxury weddings, these venues are synonymous with uniquely exquisite experiences. Known for providing flawless hospitality, elegant ballrooms, curated services, and much more, to have you feeling like royalty.


  • Convention Halls: Commonly preferred for large-scale weddings with expansive capacities, multiple event spaces, ample parking, and efficient crowd management.


  • Banquet Halls: Well-suited for indoor celebrations, offering accessibility, affordability, modern facilities, and flexible options for different wedding sizes.


Take your pick from all these kinds of wedding venues and watch the stage get set for the most memorable celebration of your life!

Best Wedding Venues Near Kempegowda International Airport


For couples planning a convenient yet impressive wedding celebration close to Kempegowda International Airport, venues like Taj Bangalore, Goldfinch Retreat Bengaluru, Signature Club Resort and Clarks Exotica Convention Resort & Spa are among the most preferred choices for their accessibility, spacious event areas, and residential wedding capabilities.

  • Taj Bangalore, Devanahalli: Situated just 300 meters from Terminal 1 and about 2.1 km from Terminal 2, this luxury hotel venue is ideal for intimate-to-mid-sized weddings with premium hospitality, elegant ballrooms, and seamless guest convenience. It is widely regarded as one of the most premium airport-side wedding venues in Bangalore.


  • Goldfinch Retreat Bengaluru, Chickajala: Located around 7 km from Bangalore Airport (roughly 10-15 minutes away), this venue is known for its convenient location, banquet and lawn spaces, guest stay options, and easy accessibility for outstation attendees.


  • Signature Club Resort, Devanahalli: Approximately 15 km away from Kempegowda International Airport, this resort-style venue is popular for intimate-to-mid-sized residential weddings thanks to its open-air spaces, accommodation options, and relaxed setting away from the city rush.


  • Clarks Exotica Convention Resort & Spa, Devanahalli: Now known as Aldovia Resort & Convention, this venue is located around 10-15 km from the Airport (approximately 17-20 minutes away). The expansive destination-style resort is known for its massive lawns, multiple banquet venues, luxury accommodation, and large-capacity wedding setups ideal for multi-day celebrations.


Wedding venues near Bangalore’s Kempegowda International Airport are especially preferred for destination-style celebrations because they reduce travel time for guests while still offering spacious lawns, banquet halls, and resort-style experiences suited for weddings of every scale. Whether you are planning an intimate residential celebration or a large multi-function wedding, these venues combine accessibility with the flexibility needed for a smooth and memorable event experience.

What to Consider Before Booking a Wedding Venue in Bengaluru


  • Date: Venues book out early in peak wedding season. Confirm your date before searching.
  • Guest count: Make sure the hall or lawn can seat all your guests comfortably.
  • Budget: Compare per-plate and rental charges across venues.
  • Location: Choose a venue close to hotels, transport, and the airport if needed.
  • Parking: Ensure enough parking space for your guest count.
  • Closing times: Many Bengaluru venues have strict sound and closing time limits. Always confirm the timing.

FAQs


What is the average cost of wedding venues in Bengaluru?

Wedding venues in Bangalore typically charge between ₹1,200 and ₹2,500 per plate, depending on the venue type, locality, and inclusions. A full one-day wedding in Bangalore usually ranges between ₹10-25 lakhs, depending on the guest count, decor, entertainment, catering, and overall scale of the celebration.

How far in advance should I book a wedding venue in Bengaluru?

The ideal time to book a wedding venue in Bangalore is around 6-8 months before the wedding. However, luxury hotels, resorts, and premium venues often get booked 9-12 months in advance during the peak wedding season from October to February and on auspicious dates. Booking early gives you better pricing, more venue options, and enough time to plan vendors and logistics comfortably.

What is the best time of year to get married in Bengaluru?

The most preferred wedding season in Bangalore is between October and February, when the weather is cooler and more comfortable for outdoor functions, lawn weddings, and resort celebrations. These months are especially popular for day weddings and multi-function destination-style events around the city outskirts.

Which are the most popular wedding venues in Bengaluru?

Some of the most popular wedding venues in Bangalore include The Leela Palace Bengaluru, ITC Gardenia, The Tamarind Tree, Royalton Leisure, and resorts near Nandi Hills. These venues are popular for their ambience, hospitality, accessibility, and suitability for large wedding celebrations.

What is typically included in a destination wedding venue package in Bengaluru?

A destination wedding venue package in Bangalore usually includes guest accommodation, meals, multiple event spaces, and basic hospitality services. Some properties may also include standard decor elements, while customised decor, entertainment, and production setups are generally charged separately. Luxury resorts may additionally provide valet parking, in-house catering, and dedicated event coordination support.

Do Bengaluru venues allow overnight weddings?

Most wedding venues within Bangalore city limits follow local sound regulations, which usually restrict loud music and DJ operations after 10 PM. However, many venues allow guests and close family members to stay overnight. Resorts and farmhouse venues located on the outskirts of Bangalore may permit extended celebrations or overnight events, subject to venue policies and local permissions.

Which localities near me in Bengaluru have the best wedding venues?

Popular localities for wedding venues in Bangalore include Whitefield, Hebbal, Yelahanka, Kanakapura Road, and Vasanth Nagar. These areas offer a mix of luxury hotels, banquet halls, resorts, convention centres, and outdoor wedding venues across different budgets and guest capacities.

How to find wedding venues in Bengaluru near me?

You can shortlist wedding venues in Bangalore by filtering options based on budget, guest capacity, locality, venue type, and event requirements on wedding planning platforms like The Wedding Company. Comparing venue pricing, reviews, photos, and inclusions before scheduling venue visits can help you finalise a venue that matches both your budget and wedding style.
