Jaipur has one of the widest venue ranges of any Indian wedding city — you can book a banquet hall for under ₹10 lakh or take over a working palace for ninety lakh and up. That range is exactly why comparing venues here gets confusing fast: a "budget" venue in one list shows up as "luxury" in another, usually because the two lists are measuring different things — venue rental per day versus catering per plate versus total wedding cost.

This page fixes that by keeping those three numbers separate and consistent throughout. If you're earlier in your planning and want the bigger picture — timing, logistics, what a Jaipur wedding week actually looks like — start with our destination wedding in Jaipur planning guide instead. This page is for once you're ready to compare specific venues.

How to read the prices on this page

Three different numbers show up when you're venue-shopping, and venues quote them inconsistently, which is exactly what caused problems on earlier versions of this page:

  • Per-plate catering cost — what you pay per guest for food, usually quoted separately for veg and non-veg
  • Per-day venue rental — what the space itself costs to book for one day, independent of food
  • Total wedding budget — rental, catering, decor, accommodation, and everything else combined, usually across a two- or three-day wedding


A palace that looks "expensive" quoted as a per-day rental can be the same venue that looks "affordable" quoted as a per-plate catering rate for a smaller guest count. Every price on this page is labeled with which of the three it refers to.

What to Consider Before Booking a Wedding Venue in Jaipur

  • Date: Book your venue, popular venues fill up months ahead.
  • Guest count: Pick a space that fits both seated and floating numbers.
  • Budget: Check per-plate and rental charges, and ask about minimums.
  • Location: Choose a venue near hotels and transport for guest convenience.
  • Parking: Verify parking capacity for cars and buses.
  • Closing times: Confirm music curfew and overnight/late-night rules.



FAQs


What's the real average cost of a Jaipur wedding venue?

Per-plate catering typically runs ₹1,500-4,500 depending on venue tier. A full two-day wedding, including venue rental, food, and basic decor, lands anywhere from ₹15 lakh at a budget property to several crore at a top palace.

Which are the most popular wedding venues in Jaipur?

Top picks include Rambagh Palace, The Oberoi Rajvilas, Samode Palace, Fairmont Jaipur, Diggi Palace, and large farmhouses around Kukas and Tonk Road.

What is the best time of year to get married in Jaipur?

The best months are October to March-end. The weather is perfectly pleasant, which makes outdoor weddings comfortable.

Do Jaipur venues allow overnight weddings?

City-center venues often have a music curfew around 10-11pm. Resorts, palaces, and farmhouses further from the city — like Mannat Marriage Garden or Shubharambh Marriage Garden — more commonly allow extended celebrations with advance approval.

Which localities near me in Jaipur have the best wedding venues?

Look in C-Scheme / Civil Lines, Amber Road / Kukas, Sanganer, Tonk Road, and the Rajasthan palace belt for top venue options.
