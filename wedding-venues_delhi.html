Types of Wedding Venues in Delhi NCR


Delhi's wedding venue market is one of the most diverse in the country. Here is a complete breakdown of every venue type available:

  • Luxury 5-Star Hotels: Ideal for premium and elegant weddings, offering sophisticated ballrooms, world-class hospitality, curated services, and refined interiors for a high-end celebration experience.


  • Banquet Halls in Delhi: Banquet halls are the most booked wedding venue type in Delhi, ideal for indoor weddings of 100 to 1,200 guests. They offer year-round reliability regardless of weather, with in-house catering, air conditioning, dedicated bridal rooms, and DJ setups. Popular banquet hall belts include Rohini, Pitampura, Janakpuri, Laxmi Nagar, and Dwarka. Pricing starts from ₹800 per plate for budget halls and goes up to ₹2,500 per plate for premium banquets with full decor packages.


Best for: Families wanting a controlled indoor environment, fixed-budget weddings, large North Delhi or West Delhi guest lists.

  • Farmhouse Wedding Venues in Delhi: Farmhouses in Chhatarpur, Mehrauli, Kapashera, and Westend Greens are the iconic choice for the classic Delhi big-fat wedding. Spanning 2 to 8 acres, these open-air venues offer full lawn access for baraats, dedicated sangeet pavilions, phera mandaps under the open sky, and complete decor flexibility. Most farmhouses include in-house catering teams and accommodate 300 to 2,000 guests. Peak-season (November–February) pricing starts from ₹8 lakh for venue-only on a Saturday.


Best for: Large guest lists, outdoor celebrations, multi-day wedding functions, baraat processions, themed decor.

  • Luxury 5-Star Wedding Hotels in Delhi: For couples who want world-class hospitality, on-site guest accommodation, and premium wedding packages all in one place, Delhi's 5-star hotels are unmatched. Properties like Taj Palace, The Leela Palace, JW Marriott Aerocity, ITC Maurya, and The Imperial offer dedicated wedding planning teams, multi-ballroom configurations, bridal suites, and curated menus. Ideal for destination-style celebrations where outstation or NRI family members need rooms on-site.


Best for: Luxury weddings, destination-style celebrations, NRI weddings, high-profile receptions.

  • Marriage Lawns & Garden Venues: Marriage lawns are open green spaces within Delhi or on its immediate outskirts, ideal for daytime ceremonies, Haldi functions, Mehendi evenings, and winter weddings. Easily decorated to any theme, they typically accommodate 200 to 800 guests and are more affordable than hotel venues. The GT Karnal Road corridor and outer South Delhi are popular lawn wedding belts.


Best for: Outdoor ceremonies, budget-conscious weddings, pre-wedding functions, winter season celebrations.

  • Wedding Resorts Near Delhi: For couples wanting a destination-wedding experience without travelling far, resorts in Manesar, Sohna Road, and the Delhi NCR periphery offer on-site accommodation, landscaped lawns, swimming pools, and banquet spaces — all under one roof. Properties like Karma Lakelands, Heritage Village Resort, and Roseate House offer complete multi-day wedding packages.


Best for: Multi-day celebrations, outstation guests, destination-feel weddings within 1–2 hours of Delhi.

  • Heritage & Palace Venues: Delhi has a growing collection of heritage properties and palace-inspired venues that bring royal grandeur to wedding celebrations. These venues offer Mughal-era architecture, ornate interiors, and bespoke event experiences perfect for heritage maximalism — the dominant wedding aesthetic trend.


Best for: Couples wanting royal aesthetics, intimate weddings, high-end editorial-style photography backdrops.

  • Convention & Community Halls: For very large weddings of 1,500 to 5,000 guests, Delhi's convention centres and community halls provide the sheer scale needed. These venues prioritise logistics and crowd management over aesthetics, but can be transformed with the right decor team.


Best for: Very large guest lists, community weddings, cost-effective large-scale events

Wedding Planning Checklist for Delhi Venues

Use this checklist when visiting and comparing venues:

Logistics

  • Is baraat entry possible? What is the driveway width?
  • Is there covered parking for 200+ cars?
  • What is the music/generator cut-off time? (Delhi noise curfew: 10 PM outdoors)
  • Is there a backup indoor space if weather changes?



Catering

  • Is outside catering permitted, or is it in-house only?
  • What cuisines does the in-house team specialise in?
  • Is there a tasting session available before booking?



Accommodation

  • How many rooms does the venue or adjacent hotel have?
  • Is there a room block arrangement for wedding guests?
  • Is the bridal suite/room included in the package?



Decor & Vendors

  • Are outside decorators permitted?
  • Is there a list of empanelled vendors, or is it open?
  • What is the decor dismantling deadline?



Legal & Contractual

  • What is the advance deposit and cancellation policy?
  • Are date-lock clauses in the contract?
  • What happens if the venue cancels due to force majeure?

What to Consider Before Booking a Wedding Venue in Delhi


  • Date: Popular venues get booked up to months in advance, so confirm your wedding date as early as possible.
  • Guest count: Choose a hall, lawn, or farmhouse that comfortably fits your guest list.
  • Budget: Check both per-plate costs and rental charges before booking.
  • Location: Pick a venue with good connectivity to hotels, the airport, and transport hubs.
  • Parking: Ensure the venue has enough parking for your guests.
  • Closing times: Most Delhi venues have music cut-off or local time restrictions. Confirm these in advance.

FAQs


What is the average cost of wedding venues in Delhi?

Wedding venues in Delhi typically charge ₹1,500- ₹3,500 per plate, with total venue costs varying by your chosen tier. A full one-day wedding in Delhi usually costs between ₹15-₹35 lakhs, depending on the guest count, decor, entertainment, catering, and overall scale of the celebration.


How far in advance should I book a wedding venue in Delhi?

The ideal time to book a wedding venue in Delhi is around 6-9 months in advance. However, luxury hotels, resorts, and premium venues often get booked 9-18 months in advance during the peak wedding season (November-February) and on auspicious dates. Booking early gives you better pricing, more venue options, and enough time to plan vendors and logistics comfortably.


What is the best time of year to get married in Delhi?

The best time to get married in Delhi is during the winter season, specifically from November to February. During these months, the cool, crisp weather makes outdoor functions and grand celebrations incredibly comfortable. These months are especially popular for day weddings and multi-function destination-style events around the city outskirts.


Which are the most popular wedding venues in Delhi?

Some of the most popular wedding venues in Delhi include The Leela Palace, ITC Maurya, The Lalit, Taj Palace, and premium outdoor spaces, such as Amaara Farms and Tivoli Gardens. These venues are popular for their ambience, hospitality, royal grandeur, and suitability for large wedding celebrations.


What is typically included in a destination wedding venue package in Delhi?

A destination wedding venue package in Delhi usually includes venue rentals, catering with multi-course menus, and guest accommodation. Some packages may also include basic event decor, while customised decor and production setups are generally charged separately. Premium packages may additionally provide entertainment (DJ and Dhol), makeup artists, and on-site event coordination.


Do Delhi venues allow overnight weddings?

Delhi venues do allow overnight wedding ceremonies and rituals to continue into the early morning. But in line with local sound regulations, outdoor amplifiers and DJs must stop by 10 PM. Events can either move to soundproof indoor halls or continue with very low, ambient background music. Many venues also allow guests and close family members to stay overnight.

Which localities near me in Delhi have the best wedding venues?

Popular localities in Delhi for wedding venues include Chanakyapuri, Chattarpur, Mehrauli, GT Karnal Road, and Aerocity. These areas offer a mix of luxury 5-star hotels, opulent banquet halls, farmhouses, private garden courtyards, and sprawling open-air lawns for different budgets and guest capacities.
